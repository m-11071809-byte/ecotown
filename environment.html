Think Green, Start Small!

Environmental problems can feel huge. But change doesn't always have to start huge.

One small action can become a habit, and one habit can inspire others. 💚
🥤

Too Much Plastic

EVERYDAY PROBLEM

Single-use plastic items can create unnecessary waste when used for only a short time.

💡 Possible Actions

  • Carry a reusable water bottle.
  • Reuse containers when practical.
  • Choose products with less unnecessary packaging.
🌍 Small change: One reusable item can replace many single-use items over time.
🍎

Food Waste

EVERYDAY PROBLEM

Food can be wasted when we take more than we need or forget about food before it is used.

💡 Possible Actions

  • Take portions you are likely to finish.
  • Check what food you already have before buying more.
  • Store food properly to help it last longer.
🍽️ Small change: Planning meals can help reduce unnecessary waste.
💡

Wasted Electricity

EVERYDAY PROBLEM

Lights and electronic devices can continue using electricity when they are not needed.

💡 Possible Actions

  • Turn off lights when leaving a room.
  • Switch off devices that are not being used.
  • Make use of natural light when practical.
⚡ Small change: Using electricity thoughtfully can reduce unnecessary energy use.
💧

Wasting Water

EVERYDAY PROBLEM

Clean water is valuable, so leaving taps running unnecessarily can waste a useful resource.

💡 Possible Actions

  • Turn off the tap when water isn't needed.
  • Report leaking taps or pipes to a responsible adult or relevant authority.
  • Use only the amount of water you need.
💧 Small change: Being mindful of water use helps avoid unnecessary waste.
🗑️

Littering

COMMUNITY PROBLEM

Rubbish left in public places can make the environment unpleasant and may harm wildlife.

💡 Possible Actions

  • Put rubbish in the appropriate bin.
  • Keep your own surroundings clean.
  • Encourage friends to care for shared spaces.
🌳 Small change: A cleaner shared space can encourage others to take better care of it too.
🌳

Losing Green Spaces

COMMUNITY PROBLEM

Trees and green spaces provide habitats for wildlife and make communities more pleasant places to live.

💡 Possible Actions

  • Take care of plants and trees around you.
  • Join suitable community gardening activities.
  • Avoid damaging plants in public spaces.
🌿 Small change: Caring for existing green spaces helps protect local habitats.
