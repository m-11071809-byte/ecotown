Community

Small acts of kindness can make a community stronger! 🌟

🏡

Better Together!

A strong community is built from people who care, communicate, and help one another.

You don't have to make a huge change. Start with one small action. 💛
🧍

Someone Feels Left Out

SOCIAL PROBLEM

Someone may feel left out when they don't have someone to talk to or join activities with.

🌱 Possible Actions

  • Invite them to join a group activity.
  • Start a friendly conversation.
  • Listen without making them feel judged.
💛 Small action: A simple invitation can help someone feel included.
🗣️

People Keep Arguing

COMMUNICATION PROBLEM

Disagreements can become bigger when people stop listening to each other.

🌱 Possible Actions

  • Give everyone a chance to speak.
  • Listen before responding.
  • Focus on solving the problem instead of blaming someone.
🗣️ Small action: Good communication can make disagreements easier to handle.
🧹

Shared Spaces Are Messy

COMMUNITY PROBLEM

Parks, classrooms, corridors, and other shared spaces can become unpleasant when people don't take care of them.

🌱 Possible Actions

  • Put rubbish in the correct bin.
  • Clean up after activities.
  • Encourage others to respect shared spaces.
🧹 Small action: Everyone doing a little can keep a shared place pleasant.
🫶

People Need Help

COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Sometimes people may need help with a task, information, or simply someone willing to listen.

🌱 Possible Actions

  • Ask if they need help.
  • Share useful information when appropriate.
  • Get a trusted adult or responsible person involved when more support is needed.
🫶 Small action: Helping someone with a simple task can make their day easier.
🎨

People Don't Connect

COMMUNITY PROBLEM

People can live or study near one another without actually getting to know each other.

🌱 Possible Actions

  • Organise a simple group activity.
  • Start a hobby or interest group.
  • Invite different people to participate.
🎨 Small action: Shared activities give people an easy reason to meet and cooperate.
💌

Not Enough Kindness

EVERYDAY PROBLEM

Busy routines can sometimes make people forget to show appreciation or kindness to others.

🌱 Possible Actions

  • Say thank you when someone helps you.
  • Give genuine encouragement.
  • Notice and appreciate someone's effort.
💌 Small action: Kind words can help create a friendlier atmosphere.
