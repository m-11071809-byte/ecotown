LEARNING

Why does active recall help?

Trying to remember information instead of simply rereading it helps strengthen your ability to retrieve that information later.

Example: close your notes and try explaining the topic from memory.
🌱
ENVIRONMENT

What is biodiversity?

Biodiversity refers to the variety of living organisms, including plants, animals and microorganisms.

Healthy ecosystems often depend on many different organisms interacting together.
💻
TECHNOLOGY

What is an algorithm?

An algorithm is a sequence of instructions used to solve a problem or complete a task.

A recipe can be thought of as a simple everyday example of an algorithm.
⚡
ENERGY

Why save electricity?

Using electricity efficiently can reduce unnecessary energy consumption.

Turning off equipment when it is not needed is one simple way to avoid unnecessary use.
💬
COMMUNICATION

What is active listening?

Active listening means paying attention, understanding the message and responding appropriately.

Asking a relevant question can show that you are paying attention.
🔐
DIGITAL SAFETY

Why are strong passwords important?

Strong, unique passwords make it harder for others to gain unauthorised access to accounts.

Using different passwords for important accounts can reduce the impact of one compromised password.
🗑️
WASTE

What is the 3R principle?

The 3Rs are Reduce, Reuse and Recycle. They encourage people to minimise waste.

Reducing consumption can often prevent waste before it is created.
🧩
PROBLEM SOLVING

Why define a problem first?

Clearly understanding a problem can help you choose a more appropriate solution.

Ask: What exactly is happening? What do I need to change? What information is missing?
