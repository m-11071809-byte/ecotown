Every Problem Has a Starting Point!

Instead of only thinking about what's wrong, try asking:

"What could I do to make this a little better?"
📚
EVERYDAY PROBLEM

"I keep forgetting my homework."

Sometimes there are too many things to remember, especially when several subjects give work at once.

🌱 Possible Solution

Keep one simple homework checklist. Write down the subject, task, and due date as soon as the teacher gives it.

⭐ Tip: Check your list before going to bed.
📱
EVERYDAY PROBLEM

"I spend too much time on my phone."

Phones are useful and entertaining, but they can easily distract us from other things.

🌱 Possible Solution

Choose specific times for checking social media or playing games. During homework or important tasks, place your phone somewhere less distracting.

⭐ Tip: Replace some screen time with a hobby, walk, drawing, reading, or chatting with someone.
😣
EVERYDAY PROBLEM

"I don't know how to deal with stress."

Schoolwork, responsibilities, and everyday situations can sometimes feel overwhelming.

🌱 Possible Solution

Break a large task into smaller steps. Focus on one thing at a time instead of trying to solve everything immediately.

⭐ Tip: Take a short break, breathe slowly, and talk to someone you trust if you need support.
🗣️
EVERYDAY PROBLEM

"I don't know how to start a difficult conversation."

It can be difficult to explain how you feel, especially when you are worried about the other person's reaction.

🌱 Possible Solution

Start calmly by explaining what happened, how you feel, and what you hope to change.

⭐ Tip: Try using "I feel..." instead of immediately blaming the other person.
🗑️
COMMUNITY PROBLEM

"There is too much rubbish around my area."

Litter can make shared spaces unpleasant and can also harm the environment.

🌱 Possible Solution

Start small. Encourage people around you to use rubbish bins, reduce unnecessary waste, and keep shared spaces clean.

⭐ Tip: A small community clean-up can make a visible difference.
⏰
EVERYDAY PROBLEM

"I always run out of time."

When everything feels urgent, it can be hard to know what to do first.

🌱 Possible Solution

Write down everything you need to do and choose the most important task first. Give yourself realistic amounts of time.

⭐ Tip: Don't try to finish everything at the same time.
